# C-teacher Video Design

## Style Prompt

C-teacher 的介绍视频应延续桌面端产品的克制、清爽、教师工作台气质：浅色画布、蓝青主色、清晰的信息层级、像真实管理软件一样的界面模块。画面应强调教师面对多个学生时的秩序感、可追踪性和本机桌面应用的可靠感。

## Colors

- Background: `#f4f8ff`
- Elevated surface: `#fbfdff`
- Primary: `#1769d4`
- Primary strong: `#0b3f8a`
- Action accent: `#11a7a2`
- Text: `#142033`
- Muted text: `#5d6b80`
- Border: `#d7e3f3`
- Warm warning: `#a86913`

## Typography

- Primary UI type: `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"SF Pro Text"`, `"Segoe UI"`, `"Noto Sans SC"`, `sans-serif`
- Headlines use 700-800 weight.
- Body and labels use 400-600 weight.

## Motion

- Calm desktop-product motion: short upward fades, horizontal panel slides, progress bars filling, check states resolving.
- Scene transitions should feel like switching clean workbench views, not cinematic or decorative.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use dark neon AI styling.
- Do not use large marketing hero cards unrelated to the app.
- Do not imply autonomous teaching or replacing teachers.
- Do not introduce feature claims beyond current README capabilities.
- Do not use stock classroom photography when the product itself can be shown through interface-like visuals.
